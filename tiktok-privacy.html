Privacy Policy - PostFlow GCL

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Who we are

PostFlow GCL is a private tool run by the owner of the "Ghien Cau Long" badminton channels. It publishes the owner's own videos, photos and posts to the owner's own accounts on TikTok, Facebook Pages and Threads. The Facebook and Threads part is described in our main privacy policy. This page covers TikTok.

2. What we collect from TikTok

  • Basic profile information of the account owner who signs in: open ID, nickname and avatar. We show them so the owner knows which account will receive a post.
  • An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok for that account.
  • The result of each publish attempt (status and post ID).

We do not collect any information about viewers, followers or other TikTok users.

3. How we use it

Only to let the account owner schedule, review and publish their own content to their own TikTok account. Videos are sent to TikTok only after the owner confirms the post.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties. Content is sent to TikTok solely to publish it at the owner's request.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

Tokens are stored privately in the owner's operating environment and are never published. They are kept only while the connection is active and are deleted when the owner disconnects TikTok. The owner can also revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Deletion requests can be sent through our Facebook Page below.
